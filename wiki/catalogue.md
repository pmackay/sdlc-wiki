The exhaustive index of every article in this wiki, one section per ontology namespace. New to the wiki? Start at the **[introduction](index.md)** instead — it explains what these namespaces mean and suggests where to begin.

Sections mirror the nine ontology namespaces — five in the **process layer** ([framework](#framework) / [capability](#capability) / [sdlc-stage](#sdlc-stage) / [artifact](#artifact) / [pattern](#pattern)), the **[harness](#harness)** layer (the agent program), the **[runtime](#runtime)** execution layer, the **[store](#store)** state layer, the **[registry](#registry)** supply layer, and the **[standard](#standard)** contract layer. A final **[topic](#topic)** section holds curated cross-cutting overlays (Maps of Content) that sit *above* the ontology graph. Schema & ontology: see [CONVENTIONS](CONVENTIONS.md).

## registry
The **supply layer** — the versioned index capabilities are discovered in, scanned, scored, evaluated, policy-gated, and installed from, *before* any run begins.
