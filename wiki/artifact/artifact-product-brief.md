# Artifact: product brief

BMAD's **pre-PRD scoping document** — `brief.md` (+ `addendum.md`), produced in the Analysis
phase to capture the problem, audience, and boundaries of a prospective project *before* the
requirements spec is written. It is one of the inputs the [[bmad-prd|PRD workflow]]
synthesizes; the brief scopes, the PRD specifies.

Two variants share this page:

- **PRFAQ** (`prfaq-{project}.md`, from [[bmad-prfaq]]) — an Amazon-style Working-Backwards
  press release + FAQ: write the launch announcement first, then stress-test whether the
  product could earn it.
- **Forged idea** (`forged-idea.md`, from [[bmad-forge-idea]]) — the hardened output of
  adversarially pressure-testing a concept "until it hardens, proves out, or dies cheaply."
