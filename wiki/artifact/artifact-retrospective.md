# Artifact: retrospective

A **human-facing learning report** produced at the close of a work period: what went well, what
didn't, and the lessons/action items future iterations should surface. gstack's [[gstack-retro]]
generates a **team-aware weekly** form — per-person breakdowns, shipping streaks, test-health
trends, growth opportunities (`/retro global` spans all projects and AI tools). BMAD's
[[bmad-retrospective]] generates the **post-epic Party-Mode** form — a facilitated persona
roundtable extracting lessons and action items.

The retrospective is the **team-process flavor** of the [[stage-learn]] stage — the human-facing
counterpart to the machine-consumable [[artifact-solution-doc]] (Compound Engineering's
agent-grounding corpus, and gstack's own [[gstack-learn]]). Same generic activity (harvest lessons
→ fold into a durable form that improves future work); different consumer (the human team vs future
agents).
