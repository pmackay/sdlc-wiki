# Artifact: RESEARCH.md / research.md

Domain / technology research output that informs the plan. Four frameworks produce it:

- **GSD — `RESEARCH.md`**: written during [[gsd-plan-phase]] by the four parallel instances of [[gsd-phase-researcher]] (stack, features, architecture, pitfalls); feeds [[gsd-planner]].
- **Spec Kit — `research.md`**: technology investigation and comparative analysis produced by [[speckit-plan]] alongside `plan.md`, `data-model.md`, and `contracts/`.
- **BMAD — research reports**: market, domain, and technical research from [[bmad-research]] (Mary), produced *up front* in the Analysis phase to ground the brief/PRD, rather than inside planning.
- **Matt Pocock — [[mp-research]]**: a background agent investigates the question against **primary sources** and writes a single **cited** Markdown file wherever the repo keeps such notes.
