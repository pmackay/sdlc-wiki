# principle-subtract-before-you-add

**Core principle.** Remove dead code, redundant validators, and stub references first, then build on the simpler base.

Applies when sequencing an addition, refactor, or rewrite. A sequencing rule rather than a sizing rule, which is what separates it from [[pstack-principle-laziness-protocol]].
