# /bro

Restates the last message in plain human language, with no jargon. The smallest skill in the plugin — its body is one sentence.

It is included in the catalogue for completeness rather than for mechanism, but it does mark a real position: pstack ships three separate prose capabilities at three registers, and this is the human-facing one. [[pstack-unslop]] is the always-on floor that removes AI tells from anything written, [[pstack-technical-writing]] is the layered standard for durable documents, and `/bro` is the on-demand translation of whatever was just said into something a person reads without decoding.
