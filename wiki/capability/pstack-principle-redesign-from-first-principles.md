# principle-redesign-from-first-principles

**Core principle.** Redesign as if the requirement had been a foundational assumption from day one, instead of bolting it on.

Applies when integrating a new requirement into an existing design. Paired with [[pstack-principle-fix-root-causes]] it is the authority [[pstack-architect]] Phase E cites when it throws the sketch out rather than absorbing more friction.
