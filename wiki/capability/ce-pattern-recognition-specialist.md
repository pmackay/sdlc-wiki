# ce-pattern-recognition-specialist

A **Code Pattern Analysis Expert** that identifies design patterns, anti-patterns, and code- quality issues across a codebase (Factory/Singleton/Observer/Strategy…), documenting where each appears and whether it is used well.

It implements [[stage-review]] (structural quality). Its most distinctive role is on the [[ce-compound]] council: on a durable-learning invocation it converts pattern analysis into **the recurring class of problem** — what pattern caused or prevented the issue, where it appears elsewhere, how the documented solution should generalize — which is the analytical core of [[pattern-knowledge-compounding]] (turning one incident into a reusable lesson). Feeds [[artifact-solution-doc]].
