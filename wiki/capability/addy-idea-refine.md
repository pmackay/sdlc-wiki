# Idea Refine

Turns a vague idea into a concrete proposal through structured **divergent then convergent**
thinking: restate the idea as a "How Might We", generate 5–8 variations via lenses
(inversion, simplification, 10x, audience shift), then cluster, stress-test against
user-value / feasibility / differentiation, and surface the hidden assumptions that usually
kill ideas. It ships a one-pager whose most valuable section is the explicit "Not Doing" list.

Distinctively, this is front-of-funnel ideation with **no direct GSD or Matt Pocock
counterpart** — where [[addy-interview-me]] narrows toward a single confirmed intent, idea-refine
deliberately widens the search first. Its closest cross-framework kin are OpenSpec's explore and
BMAD's [[bmad-brainstorming]] (diverge-then-converge ideation). It implements [[stage-align]].
