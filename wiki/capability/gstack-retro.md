# /retro

`/retro` — the **Eng Manager's** team-aware **weekly retrospective**: per-person breakdowns,
shipping streaks, test-health trends, and growth opportunities. `/retro global` runs across all your
projects and AI tools (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini). Produces the retro report ([[artifact-retrospective]]).

gstack's **team-process** flavor of [[stage-learn]] — the direct counterpart to BMAD's
[[bmad-retrospective]] (a human-facing roundtable extracting lessons for future sprints). gstack's
**agent-grounding** flavor is [[gstack-learn]]. Having both is what gives gstack a full presence in
the learn stage and makes it the **third framework** (with Compound Engineering and BMAD) to
evidence it — see [[stage-learn]].
